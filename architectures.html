Architecture Encyclopedia

A map of the architectural ideas that shape modern foundation models—from attention and Transformers to mixture-of-experts, state space models, recurrent alternatives, diffusion, and emerging hybrids.

Architectures, not just model names.

The same architectural idea can appear across many different model families. The Atlas therefore treats architecture as its own knowledge layer, connecting concepts to models, papers, evolution, computational characteristics, and practical tradeoffs.
