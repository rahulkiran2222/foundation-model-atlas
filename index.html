06 / Evolution

Models as lineages

The long-term goal is to connect architecture ideas, families, generations, variants, successors, and major technical changes.

2017 →

Attention becomes a general architectural foundation

The Transformer establishes a reusable sequence-modeling pattern that later branches into many model families and modalities.

2019 →

Decoder-only language modeling scales

Autoregressive language models become a major foundation for modern generative systems.

2020s →

Sparsity, long-context methods, and multimodality diversify the stack

MoE routing, efficient attention, alternative sequence mixers, multimodal interfaces, and specialized model families expand the design space.

Atlas direction →

Architecture becomes explorable data

The Atlas connects model records to machine-readable topology so users can study how systems are assembled, not only what they are called.

07 / Methodology

Evidence before certainty

The Atlas should distinguish verified fields from generalized representations and unavailable information.

Primary-source hierarchy

Architecture and model claims should lead back to official papers, model cards, repositories, documentation, or other authoritative sources. Secondary sources can help discovery but should not silently replace primary evidence.
