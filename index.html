05 / Methodology

Built as a research resource

The Atlas prioritizes provenance, clarity, reproducibility, and primary sources.

Read methodology →
01

Primary sources first

Architecture and model claims should be traced to official papers, model cards, repositories, documentation, or other authoritative sources.

02

Unknown is acceptable

Missing information is represented as unknown rather than inferred or fabricated.

03

Versioned knowledge

Model information changes. The Atlas should preserve provenance and make updates traceable over time.

Every model should lead back to evidence.

The Atlas is designed around a source hierarchy that connects model entries to the research ecosystem behind them. Where available, primary sources take precedence over secondary summaries.
